# Luminous Technologies 🚜

### Construction Equipment Rental & Sales Website

A professional business website for Luminous Technologies, focused on construction equipment rental and sales services.

---

## 📌 About the Company

Luminous Technologies is a business focused on construction equipment and machinery services.

This website aims to provide customers with information about available construction equipment, rental services, purchasing opportunities, and company contact details.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Establish a professional online presence for Luminous Technologies.
- Showcase construction equipment and machinery.
- Provide rental and purchasing information.
- Make it easier for customers to contact the company.
- Develop a user-friendly website.
- Build a foundation for future business management features.
